Technology Platforms

ATLAS scales spatial cell mapping using learned transcriptional signatures

ATLAS learns signatures from single-cell RNA-seq references and decodes them from tissue measurements to infer cell types and transcriptomes. Profiling millions of mouse-brain cells demonstrates comparative mapping across genetic backgrounds, while imputed expression should be distinguished from directly measured genes.

CCI-seq connects cell proximity with molecular states

CCI-seq combines indexing of cell clumps with single-cell sequencing to identify proximity networks and associated molecular states. Applications in mouse tissues and human colorectal cancer show how it can complement transcriptomic studies of tissue and tumor microenvironments.

Matched 10x and Parse comparisons reveal platform-dependent RNA profiles

This preprint compares seven matched sample pairs from five studies and separates priming-related biases from larger platform-level differences between 10x and Parse. Reported differences in nuclear RNA representation and comparable background contamination caution against treating measurements as interchangeable; the study focuses on gene counts and composition rather than a universal platform ranking.

OpenDVP links image analysis and spatial proteomics in archival tissues

OpenDVP combines open image-analysis tools with AnnData and SpatialData in an experimental and computational workflow for deep visual proteomics. Demonstrations in placenta, lung cancer and relapsed breast cancer connect spatial cell phenotypes with exploratory proteomic measurements and neighborhood profiling.

Spatial transcriptomics benchmark compares capture and spatial accuracy

This preprint compares sequencing-based spatial transcriptomics platforms across tissue types and preservation methods, examining capture architecture, capture efficiency and spatial accuracy. Its proposed operational metrics may help experimental planning, but performance remains dependent on the tested assays and specimens.

Ambient RNA correction can conceal doublets in single-cell analysis

This preprint evaluates ambient RNA correction and doublet detection using engineered and multiplexed datasets. Doublet detection generally worked better before decontX or SoupX correction, with CellBender an exception, suggesting that preprocessing order should be validated rather than assumed interchangeable.

Cell2space infers tissue domains and neighborhoods for dissociated cells

RNA-seq Blog covers Cell2space, which learns spatial affinity from a spatial-transcriptomics reference and reconstructs cell domains and neighborhood relationships stepwise. Demonstrations in mouse cortex and human skin illustrate inferred spatial context, which should not be treated as direct measurement of each dissociated cell’s location.

reference mappingsingle cellspatial transcriptomics

CellART combines segmentation and annotation in spatial transcriptomics

CellART integrates tissue images, spatial measurements and scRNA-seq references to recover cell boundaries and assign cell identities. RNA-seq Blog highlights analyses including breast cancer, with the inferred profiles and labels dependent on reference quality and the spatial platform.

CellDiffusion integrates single-cell studies using graph diffusion

CellDiffusion learns relationships among cells to correct batch effects while preserving cell-type structure and trajectories. RNA-seq Blog covers the October 16 Science Advances paper, whose evaluations include human endometrium data; reported performance remains specific to the tested datasets.

DeCoRE resolves RNA conformations using direct RNA sequencing

DeCoRE combines RNA structure probing with direct RNA sequencing signals to separate alternative structural conformations. Demonstrations on riboswitches and bacterial ribosomal RNA suggest a route to studying structural heterogeneity, with conclusions limited to the evaluated experimental systems.

DeepDiff classifies early developmental states using a curated marker panel

RNA-seq Blog describes a marker framework validated across stem-cell experiments and public transcriptomic datasets, with DeepDiff providing a web interface for early fate classification. Its value is in evaluating developmental identity across assays, with marker specificity still dependent on biological context.

HitSV detects structural variants across sequencing technologies

HitSV combines repeat-aware breakpoint detection with haplotype-resolved local assembly to reconstruct and genotype structural variants from short, long or hybrid reads. Reported benchmark and cohort results make it a candidate for variant-pipeline evaluation, with performance dependent on the sequencing and genomic contexts tested.

Nanopore RNA motif analysis prioritizes modification candidates

RNA-seq Blog covers an approach that analyzes direct-RNA sequencing predictions to nominate sequence motifs associated with pseudouridine, inosine and m5C. Results from cell-line and blood data prioritize candidate contexts for biological validation rather than proving new modification sites.

SpliceCOV filters transcriptional noise before transcript assembly

SpliceCOV uses annotated splice and transcript-boundary features to filter noisy RNA-seq alignments before StringTie assembly. RNA-seq Blog covers the October 20 Genes paper, which reports improved precision in GTEx comparisons but weaker transfer to mouse data, highlighting the importance of organism-specific validation.

VIMA tests disease-associated microniches in spatial molecular data

VIMA learns continuous tissue-patch representations and tests overlapping microniches for associations with case–control status, avoiding reliance on a fixed set of cell types or tissue regions. Simulations and applications across three diseases evaluate calibration and power, offering a framework for study-level spatial comparisons.

AmpliconSuite organizes whole-genome sequencing analysis of focal amplifications, including extrachromosomal DNA and breakage–fusion–bridge structures. JEEPERS identifies and corrects methylation errors introduced when jagged cell-free DNA ends are repaired during library preparation. Evaluations in cancer-related profiling illustrate joint methylation and mutation analysis, but clinical deployment requires further validation and the software carries a noncommercial research license.

CauTrigger prioritizes candidate drivers of biological state transitions

CauTrigger uses hierarchical latent-variable modeling to separate proposed causal signals from confounding variation and predict perturbation outcomes across regulatory layers. Applications to single-cell multiomics generate testable hypotheses; causal interpretation remains dependent on modeling assumptions and experimental follow-up.

DynaVelo learns joint RNA-expression and transcription-factor motif dynamics with a neural differential-equation model, enabling in silico perturbations of germinal-center B cells.
